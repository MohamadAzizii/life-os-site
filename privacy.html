Life OS privacy policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

Life OS is a private dashboard that runs on its owner's own Mac. It is not offered to the public. This page explains what it does with data from Google accounts that are connected to it.

What Life OS accesses

When someone connects a Google account, Life OS asks for permission to:

  • Read Gmail: to find bills, deadlines, appointments, renewals and deliveries, and to see which conversations are waiting for a reply.
  • Send Gmail: only to send a reply that the owner has written or reviewed and explicitly confirmed. Life OS never sends email on its own.
  • Read Google Calendar: to show the day's events on one timeline.
  • Basic profile (email address): to know which account is connected.

Where the data goes

  • Everything Life OS keeps is stored only on the owner's own computer. There is no Life OS server or cloud database.
  • To pick out bills and deadlines, the text of a relevant email is sent to Anthropic's Claude AI service and the extracted result comes back. Newsletters and promotional mail are filtered out before this step. The data is not used to train AI models.
  • Google data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never shared with anyone else.

How long it is kept

Life OS keeps the items it finds (for example "British Gas bill £84.20, due 18 November") until they are dismissed. A short snippet and a link to the original message are kept. Full message text is deleted after 90 days. The original email always stays in Gmail.

Removing access

Any connected account can be disconnected inside Life OS at any time. Access can also be revoked from the Google account at myaccount.google.com/connections. After that, Life OS can no longer read or send anything for that account.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Life OS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
